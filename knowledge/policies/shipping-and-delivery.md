# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Service levels

We offer two delivery speeds within serviceable pin codes:

- Standard delivery: 5 to 7 business days, free on orders above ₹499
- Express delivery: 2 to 3 business days, ₹79 flat

Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST on a business day are dispatched the same day.
Orders placed after the cut-off, on Sundays, or on national holidays are
dispatched the next business day.

## Tracking orders

Every dispatched order receives a tracking number by email and SMS. Customers
can track an order from the "My Orders" page or by using the tracking number on
the courier's website. Tracking updates can lag by up to 12 hours after dispatch.

If the tracking status has not changed for more than 72 hours, support should
raise a courier escalation; the courier is expected to respond within 24 hours.

## Delivery attempts

Couriers make up to three delivery attempts on consecutive business days. After
the third failed attempt the parcel is returned to the nearest warehouse and the
order is marked "returned to origin". Customers can request redelivery within 15
days; a redelivery fee of ₹79 applies.

## Delayed deliveries

An order is considered late when it has not been delivered within 3 business
days of the promised delivery date. For late orders customers may choose either:

- A ₹100 store credit, or
- Cancellation with a full refund, with no return shipping fee

## Serviceable areas

We currently ship to about 19,000 pin codes across India. International shipping
is not available. If a pin code is not serviceable, the order cannot be placed
and customers are advised to use an alternate address.

## Lost parcels

A parcel is treated as lost if the courier confirms it cannot be located, or if
tracking shows no movement for 10 business days. Lost parcels are refunded in
full or replaced at the customer's choice; the customer is not charged any fee.
