# Order Cancellation Policy

## Cancelling before dispatch

Orders can be cancelled free of charge any time before dispatch. Cancellations
are confirmed instantly from the "My Orders" page, and the refund is initiated
immediately.

## Cancelling after dispatch

Once an order is dispatched it can no longer be cancelled in the app. Customers
have two options:

- Refuse delivery when the courier arrives, after which the parcel returns to the
  warehouse and a full refund is issued, or
- Accept the delivery and raise a standard return within 30 days

## Refund timing for cancellations

Refunds for cancelled orders follow the normal refund timeline: UPI and wallet
within 2 business days, cards within 5 to 7 business days, net banking within 7 to
10 business days. The refund is always issued to the original payment method.

## Cancelling a subscription

Product orders and subscriptions are separate. For subscription cancellation see
the Billing and Subscription policy. Cancelling a product order never cancels a
subscription.

## Cancellation fees

There is no cancellation fee for orders cancelled before dispatch. For orders
refused after dispatch on a cash-on-delivery order, no fee applies. Prepaid orders
refused after dispatch are refunded in full less any non-refundable payment
gateway charge, which is currently ₹0.

## Repeated cancellations

Accounts that cancel more than 8 orders in a rolling 30-day period are flagged
for review. Flagged accounts may require manual approval for new orders. This is a
fraud-prevention measure and support should not promise an exception.
