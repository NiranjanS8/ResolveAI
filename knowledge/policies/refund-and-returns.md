# Refund and Return Policy

## Eligibility window

Customers may request a refund or return within 30 days of delivery. The window
is measured from the delivery date recorded on the order, not the order date.
Items must be unused and in original packaging with all accessories, tags and
manuals included.

## Non-returnable items

The following are not eligible for refund or return:

- Personal care and hygiene products that have been opened
- Perishable goods and groceries
- Gift cards and prepaid vouchers
- Items marked "final sale" on the product page
- Digital downloads once the download has started

## How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. UPI and wallet
refunds complete within 2 business days. Card refunds take 5 to 7 business days
depending on the issuing bank. Net banking refunds take 7 to 10 business days.

Customers receive an email confirmation as soon as the refund is initiated. If
the refund has not appeared after the stated window, support may raise a refund
trace with the payment gateway; traces take up to 5 additional business days.

## Automatic approval threshold

Refunds of ₹2,000 or less for a verified order may be approved without human
review, provided the request falls inside the eligibility window and the item is
not on the non-returnable list. Refunds above ₹2,000 always require a support
agent to review and approve before the refund is issued.

## Damaged or incorrect items

If an item arrives damaged, defective or different from what was ordered,
customers should report it within 7 days of delivery with photographs. In these
cases the customer is not charged return shipping, and a replacement or full
refund is offered at the customer's choice. Damaged-item reports are prioritised
and do not count against the standard return window.

## Return shipping

For standard change-of-mind returns, a flat return shipping fee of ₹99 is
deducted from the refund. Return shipping is free for damaged, defective or
incorrect items, and for orders that were delivered later than the promised
delivery date.
