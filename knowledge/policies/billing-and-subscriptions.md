# Billing and Subscription Policy

## Billing cycle

Subscriptions are billed monthly on the anniversary of the sign-up date. If a
subscription started on the 31st and a month has fewer days, the charge moves to
the last day of that month. Annual plans are billed once every 12 months.

Invoices are emailed on the billing date and are always available under
Settings → Billing → Invoices. Invoices are generated in the customer's local
currency at the exchange rate on the billing date.

## Failed payments

When a recurring charge fails, we retry on days 1, 3 and 5 after the due date.
The subscription stays active during the retry window. If all retries fail, the
subscription is suspended and the customer keeps read-only access for 14 days
before the account is downgraded to the free tier.

Common failure reasons are insufficient funds, expired cards, and bank blocks on
recurring mandates. Support can resend a payment link so the customer can pay
manually; a manual payment reactivates the subscription immediately.

## Plan changes

Upgrades take effect immediately and the price difference is charged on a
pro-rated basis for the remainder of the current period. Downgrades take effect
at the start of the next billing cycle. Downgrading does not trigger a refund for
the current period.

## Cancellation and refunds for subscriptions

Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time from Settings → Billing. Cancellation
stops future charges and access continues until the end of the paid period.
Subscription fees are refunded only when:

- The customer was charged twice for the same period, or
- The customer cancelled within 7 days of the first charge of a new annual plan
  and has not used the paid features

Monthly subscription fees are generally not refunded for partial periods.

## Taxes

Prices displayed include GST. Business customers can add a GSTIN to their billing
profile; invoices are reissued with the GSTIN within 1 business day.

## Duplicate and incorrect charges

Suspected duplicate charges should be verified against the invoice list before a
refund is raised. If a genuine duplicate is confirmed, refund it in full
immediately without waiting for gateway settlement. Incorrect charge amounts are
escalated to the billing team with the invoice number.
