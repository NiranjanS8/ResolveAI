# Escalation and Service Level Agreements

## When to escalate

Escalate a ticket to a human agent when:

- The request is ambiguous and the customer's intent cannot be determined
- The refund amount exceeds the ₹2,000 automatic approval threshold
- Identity verification for an account or 2FA change cannot be completed
- The customer explicitly asks to speak to a person
- The issue is a suspected security or fraud incident
- The automated draft would require a commitment we cannot keep, such as an
  exception to policy or a delivery date a courier has not confirmed

Escalating is the correct action when confidence is low. It is always preferable
to escalate than to send a customer an incorrect automated answer.

## Priority levels

- P1 Critical: security compromise, fraud, or a site-wide outage. First response
  within 1 hour.
- P2 High: a paid order or subscription is blocked. First response within 4 hours.
- P3 Normal: standard order, refund, billing or account questions. First response
  within 24 hours.
- P4 Low: general questions and feedback. First response within 48 hours.

## What to include in an escalation

An escalation should carry the ticket summary, the customer's own words, the
order number if relevant, any knowledge-base excerpts that were retrieved, and a
short note on what the agent already told the customer. Missing context is the
most common cause of a slow resolution.

## Response time expectations

First response is measured from when the customer sends the message. Resolution
time targets are 1 business day for P2, 3 business days for P3 and 5 business days
for P4. P1 is worked continuously until the incident is closed.

Business hours are 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM IST, seven days a week. Responses outside
those hours, and on public holidays, may take longer, and agents should not promise
a specific clock time outside business hours.

## Automatic draft reviews

Drafts written by the automated agent are reviewed by a human before they are sent
whenever the routing confidence is medium, or the intent involves a refund,
account, cancellation or billing decision above the auto-approval threshold.
Reviewers may edit the draft; if an edit is substantial, the corrected response is
fed back into the knowledge base to improve future drafts.

## Complaints about a previous interaction

Complaints about a previous agent or a previous resolution are P2 and must be
handled by a human. Apologise for the experience, gather the ticket numbers
involved, and route the complaint to the support lead rather than replying with an
automated draft.
